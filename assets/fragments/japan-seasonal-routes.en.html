Planning tips

  • Cherry blossom forecasts come out around January–February and foliage forecasts around September. Lock in your dates once they are out.
  • Winter schedules start in late October and some routes lose flights (Aomori drops to three a week, for example); Asahikawa is seasonal. Check the airline's website before booking.
  • To drive, bring your original Taiwanese license plus the Japanese translation issued by the Motor Vehicles Office. From December to March, public transport is the safer choice in Hokkaido, Tohoku, Hokuriku and Shinshu.
  • Avoid Japanese holidays: Golden Week (late April–early May), Obon (mid August) and New Year (December 29–January 3).
  • Book ahead: Ghibli Park, Chichu Art Museum on Naoshima, Kawachi Fujien, Shirakawa-go light-up days, the Harry Potter studio tour, PokéPark KANTO, and popular Disney and USJ time slots.
  • Island-hopping combos (such as Okinawa Main Island to Ishigaki) need a separate Japanese domestic flight.
